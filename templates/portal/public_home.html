How a report travels

  • REPORTED — the portal records it and replies in your language within seconds.
  • VERIFIED — a volunteer, NGO coordinator or district officer confirms it on site.
  • PRIORITISED — it enters the ranked queue with a service deadline.
  • ASSIGNED & IN TRANSIT — a depot cuts the kit and a vehicle, boat or porter leaves.
  • DELIVERED — handover is recorded with an OTP or a photograph.

What we ask for, and what we never ask for

We ask how many people are in the household and how many of them are infants, elderly, pregnant, disabled or on regular medication. Those counts are verifiable at the door and they decide the size of the kit.

We never ask for caste, religion, income or an Aadhaar number. None of those fields exist in this system.
